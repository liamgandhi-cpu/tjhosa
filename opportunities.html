High School Student Volunteer Program

Inova Fairfax Medical Campus

What you’ll do
Support hospital departments, help patients and visitors, and learn how a hospital runs.
Eligibility
At least 16 by the start of your session and interested in a healthcare career. Requires a 250-word response, an information meeting with a parent, a background check, TB screening, and vaccination records.
Location
Falls Church, VA (in person)
Deadline
Session-based. Check Inova’s page for current session dates.
View Inova Volunteer Info

Youth Volunteer Opportunities

American Red Cross

What you’ll do
Organize blood drives, recruit donors, join a youth club, or help donors as a Blood Donor Ambassador.
Eligibility
Many donor-facing roles require age 16+. Youth club ages vary.
Location
Northern Virginia (varies by drive or chapter)
Deadline
Rolling. Apply online anytime.

Aspiring Scientists Summer Internship Program (ASSIP)

George Mason University

What you’ll do
Do a mentored summer research project with a Mason faculty member, analyze data, and present at a symposium.
Eligibility
High school students, 15+ (16+ for wet labs). There’s a $25 application fee and tuition for accepted interns, and fee waivers are available.
Location
Fairfax, Manassas, and other Mason campuses (in person, varies by lab)
Deadline
2027 deadline not posted yet. The 2026 deadline was February 15.
View ASSIP Application Info

Summer High School Volunteer Program

Children’s National Hospital

What you’ll do
Six weeks of hospitality volunteering, healthcare workshops, and a group project at the main hospital.
Eligibility
15+ by the start of the program and returning to high school in the fall (no graduating seniors).
Location
Washington, DC (in person)
Deadline
The 2027 application opens in February 2027.
