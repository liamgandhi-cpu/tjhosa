Research Opportunities

University labs that have agreed to take TJ HOSA members, and how to apply.

How to apply

These professors told us they’re open to taking TJ HOSA members. Apply through the chapter, not by emailing professors yourself. We send every application for a lab to its professor, and they choose who fits best.

  1. 1

    Pick a lab

    Read what each lab expects, especially hours and timing. Apply to each lab separately.

  2. 2

    Fill out the lab’s form

    Press Apply on the lab’s card. Each lab has its own application form, so fill one out for every lab you want. Have anything listed under “Also include” ready.

  3. 3

    We send it to the professor

    We pass every application along to the lab. Questions? Email tjhssthosa@gmail.com.

Application deadlines: Details coming soon. Dr. Cho’s lab needs applications before March 31, 2027.
