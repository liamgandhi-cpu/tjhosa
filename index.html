What Is TJ HOSA?

TJ HOSA is our school’s chapter of HOSA–Future Health Professionals. We bring together students interested in health and medicine through competitions, hands-on activities, service, leadership, and opportunities to meet health professionals. This website brings our chapter’s information and resources together in one place.
