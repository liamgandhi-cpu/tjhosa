    History of TJ HOSA

    Shut down by a rule. Brought back by a law.

    Most clubs are founded. Ours was founded twice: the second time came after TJ students went to Richmond and changed the rule for every public school in Virginia.

    The first chapter

    TJ used to have a HOSA chapter. Members competed, studied health careers, and served the community like chapters across the state.

    [Photo + founding year of the original chapter, and any alumni results]

    The rule that ended it

    Virginia tied career and technical student organizations (CTSOs) like HOSA to career and technical education courses. To have a chapter, a school had to offer a qualifying course. TJ, the state's top science and technology school, didn't offer the qualifying health science course, so the chapter lost its charter.

    Interest, performance, and the number of students headed into medicine had nothing to do with it. The chapter closed because of one line in an eligibility rule.

    Going to Richmond

    The students didn't ask for an exception for one school. They argued that no school's course catalog should decide whether its students can join a national professional organization. They researched the code section, built the case, and found legislators willing to carry the bill.

    [Names of the students involved, the bill's patron, and photos from the General Assembly or testimony]
    “Each public middle school and high school may establish career and technical education student organizations, regardless of whether such school offers career and technical education courses.”Va. Code § 22.1-227.1, as amended by SB 707 · 2024 Acts of Assembly, ch. 776

    What changed

    SB 707 was signed on April 8, 2024, and took effect July 1, 2024. It opened HOSA, DECA, FBLA, TSA, SkillsUSA, FCCLA, and Educators Rising to every public middle and high school in Virginia, including the ones that had been locked out.

    Chartered again

    This is why TJ HOSA has no course prerequisite, no application, and no tryout. If you go to TJ, you can join. The students who fixed the rule passed the chapter on to the next class, and the 2026–27 chapter is the first full roster since.

    Questions

    Do I have to compete?

    No. Competing is one way to take part. You can also come to 8th period, sign up for opportunities, and help run events.

    Do I need an 8th-period spot to compete?

    No. Any affiliated member can compete. Most competition prep happens outside 8th period, with help from your event lead.

    Can I switch my competitive event?

    Yes, as long as there's room. Submit a switch request in the Member Hub. Competition Directors check capacity and team rules and get back to you by email.

    How do shadowing and research matches work?

    For "Match" listings, you submit your interests and resume. Officers choose one student per opening and send a single introduction, which keeps hosts from getting flooded with emails. Please don't contact hosts directly.

    I'm not pre-med. Is this for me?

    Yes. HOSA has events and opportunities in public health, biomedical engineering, health policy, psych and neuro, nutrition, veterinary science, and more.

    What if I don't get an 8th-period spot?

    Spots reset every week, so try again at the next sign-up.
